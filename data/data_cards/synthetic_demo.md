# Data Card: HeatSafe Synthetic Demonstration

- **Purpose:** deterministic offline tests, tutorials, UI demonstrations, and CPU benchmark smoke tests.
- **Status:** entirely synthetic; not suitable for scientific conclusions about any named city.
- **Coverage:** illustrative hourly heat/air-quality series, daily climate series, urban-grid cells, fire detections, and a home log.
- **Generation:** fixed random seed in `scripts/generate_demo_data.py`.
- **Limitations:** seasonal patterns and correlations are intentionally simplified. Do not compare them with real measurements.
