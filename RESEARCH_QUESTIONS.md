# Research Questions

1. How much does compound heat and PM2.5 forecasting improve when meteorology, smoke context, urban form and temporal history are modeled jointly?
2. Which forecast baselines remain strongest under city and regional domain shift?
3. How rapidly does calibration degrade during extreme heat or smoke episodes?
4. Can adaptive conformal methods maintain interval coverage under seasonal and geographic shift?
5. How sensitive are compound-risk rankings to weights, thresholds and missing variables?
6. Which features consistently improve prediction across Europe, North America and Türkiye?
7. How should satellite land-surface temperature and station air temperature be fused without conflating their physical meanings?
8. What is the predictive value of hot-night persistence for next-day indoor heat stress?
9. Can household thermal lag be estimated robustly from sparse, user-entered observations?
10. Under what conditions does ventilation reduce indoor heat while maintaining acceptable outdoor-air quality?
11. How do data-source disagreement and model-grid resolution affect local decisions?
12. Which local or open-source language models can explain deterministic scientific outputs without altering measurements or conclusions?
13. Can retrieval-augmented explanation improve documentation quality while preserving source traceability?
14. What failure modes arise when optional cloud AI is used for scientific narration?
15. Which reproducibility metadata are necessary to recreate a result months or years later?
