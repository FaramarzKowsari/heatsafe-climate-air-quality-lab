## Scientific safeguards

- Deterministic functions produce primary decisions and metrics.
- Language-model modes only explain already-computed results.
- Satellite land-surface temperature remains distinct from air temperature.
- Data measurement types remain explicit.
- Wildfire context does not claim definitive source attribution.
- AQI conversion is labeled by standard.
- Household tools do not produce certified building or health scores.
- Observational comparisons do not claim controlled causation.
- No medical diagnosis, official warning or guaranteed outcome is claimed.
