# Data Sources

HeatSafe distinguishes **observed**, **satellite-derived**, **reanalysis**, **modeled**, **forecast**, **user-entered**, **estimated**, and **synthetic** records. The categories are never presented as interchangeable.

The current research preview has live adapters for Open-Meteo weather and air-quality forecasts, NASA POWER daily modeled meteorology, and OpenAQ v3 observations when a user supplies a key. Adapter specifications are included for NOAA CDO, EPA AQS, Copernicus ERA5-Land, NASA FIRMS, EEA air quality, and ECCC GeoMet.

See [`data/catalog.yml`](data/catalog.yml) and [`DATA_LICENSES.md`](DATA_LICENSES.md). A connector being listed does not mean that GitHub redistributes its source data.
