A SIMPLE PATH

From observation to a practical action

  1. 1
    Observe

    Check the indoor condition and the latest outdoor heat and air-quality information.

  2. 2
    Compare

    Ask whether outdoor air is cooler, cleaner or more humid than the indoor environment.

  3. 3
    Choose

    Select a reversible action such as shade, ventilation, a cooler room, a fan or scheduled cooling.

  4. 4
    Record

    Note what happened so future decisions are based on observations rather than memory alone.

  5. 5
    Escalate when needed

    Use trusted local alerts and qualified professional support whenever conditions or symptoms are serious.

PUBLIC-INTEREST PRINCIPLES

What this page does—and does not—do

Free access

No purchase button, subscription, affiliate link or product recommendation.

Visible assumptions

Tools show the values and thresholds behind results instead of presenting a hidden score.

Local-first use

Manual entry remains available, and several activities store information only in the user’s browser.

No false authority

Results are educational aids, not official forecasts, medical instructions, emergency warnings or certified reports.

!
IMPORTANT SAFETY LIMIT

Use official local guidance when risk is high.

Extreme heat and smoke can become urgent, especially for older adults, infants, people with chronic illness, people taking certain medicines, outdoor workers and anyone without effective cooling or clean air. This website cannot evaluate a person’s medical condition or replace local emergency services.
