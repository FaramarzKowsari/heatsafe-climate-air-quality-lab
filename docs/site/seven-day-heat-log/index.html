A USEFUL RHYTHM

Record at consistent moments.

  • Morning before strong sun
  • Mid-afternoon near peak indoor heat
  • Evening before ventilation
  • About 30–60 minutes after a major action
  • Before sleep and after waking
Privacy: all entries remain in your browser unless you export them.
Tip: the 24-Hour Planner can use your most recent log entry to prefill current indoor temperature, humidity and PM2.5.
Important: use official local warnings and seek qualified help for dangerous heat, smoke, electrical or building conditions.
