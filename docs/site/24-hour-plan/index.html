WHAT THE PLANNER BALANCES

Heat removal, heat prevention and clean air.

  • Use cooler hours before the home stores heat
  • Block sun before windows and rooms become hot
  • Avoid ventilation when outdoor air is entered as poor
  • Protect sleep and create one priority room
  • Escalate to official guidance when conditions become dangerous
