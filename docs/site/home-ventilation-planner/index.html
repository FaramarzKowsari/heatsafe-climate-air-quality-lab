DECISION LOGIC

Heat benefit must be balanced with outdoor air.

  • Outdoor air must be cooler than indoor air to remove heat.
  • PM2.5 and likely smoke can override cooling potential.
  • High outdoor humidity can reduce comfort benefit.
  • Very weak wind and single-sided ventilation may slow exchange.
  • High daytime solar gain can continue adding heat.
Educational defaults: PM2.5 thresholds of 12, 35 and 55 µg/m³; useful temperature advantage 1.5°C; strong advantage 3°C.
Privacy: no forecast or household data is uploaded by this page.
Important: thresholds may differ from official local guidance. Check trusted local heat, smoke and air-quality information.
