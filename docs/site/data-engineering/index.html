PRODUCTION-GRADE DATA ENGINEERING

Every result begins with data identity.

HeatSafe records where environmental data came from, how it was retrieved, what it measures, which terms apply, how quality was assessed and whether the exact snapshot can be verified later.

Typed contracts

Observed, forecast, modeled, reanalysis, satellite and synthetic records remain distinct.

Retries, rate limiting, cache TTL, response hashes and metadata.

Quality reports

Duplicates, ranges, units, source URLs, licenses and provider flags.

Verifiable snapshots

Atomic JSONL artifacts, manifests, SHA-256 and record-count verification.
