HOW TO USE IT

Measure before and after actions.

  • Record a baseline before changing windows, shade, fan or cooling.
  • Record again after 30–60 minutes.
  • Repeat under similar conditions on several days.
  • Use the same sensor location where possible.
  • Interpret action responses as observational, not controlled experiments.
Privacy: entries stay in your browser unless you export them.
Thermal lag: the tool estimates delay by testing time shifts between outdoor and indoor temperature series. Sparse or irregular data reduces confidence.
Important: sensor error, sunlight on sensors, cooking, occupancy and changing weather can distort apparent effects.
