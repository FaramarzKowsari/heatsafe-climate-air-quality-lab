GEOGRAPHIC ROBUSTNESS LABORATORY

Can the model travel?

This laboratory holds out complete cities and regions, measures domain shift, compares forecasts statistically and publishes worst-domain performance—not only average scores.

Leave one city out

A complete city is excluded from fitting and uncertainty calibration.

Leave one region out

Every city in one region becomes an untouched external test domain.

Shift diagnostics

Target shift, feature shift and event-rate shift accompany every fold.

Block bootstrap

Paired skill uncertainty is estimated without destroying short temporal dependence.

Forecast comparison

Horizon-aware Diebold–Mariano statistics compare candidates with persistence.

Robust leaderboard

Ranking includes mean error, worst-domain error, event skill and interval calibration.
