# Regional Workflows

Regional templates are configuration examples, not fixed conclusions. Türkiye receives dedicated examples for Istanbul, Ankara, İzmir, Antalya, Adana, Bursa, Mediterranean Türkiye, Central Anatolia, and Eastern Anatolia. Europe is divided into northern, central, Mediterranean, and humid-coastal contexts. North America includes western/eastern United States, Canada, wildfire-smoke-prone areas, and hot urban interiors.
